SIGNUM — DOCUMENT AND AI RISK NOTICE

Read this notice before installing the application.

The program is educational and is not suitable for commercial use. Use it only
with sample documents that contain no personal or sensitive data.

1. PURPOSE AND LIMITATIONS

Signum is a tool that assists with finding indications of signatures, initials,
and stamps in documents. Visual-analysis results are generated by an AI model
and may be incorrect, incomplete, or misleading.

A detected signature does not confirm the signer's identity, the signature's
authenticity, its legal or cryptographic validity, or the document's integrity.
The application does not validate certificates, revocation, or the full
correctness of a digital signature. Every result requires human verification
against the source document and should not be the sole basis for a legal,
business, or organizational decision.

2. DOCUMENTS, AUTHORIZATION, AND CONFIDENTIALITY

Documents may contain personal, confidential, privileged, or other sensitive
data. Signum cannot determine whether the user may legally and safely process
the selected files. Before analysis, the user must assess required
authorizations, organizational policies, purpose, data minimization, and
safeguards. The user is also responsible for deciding whether a result is fit
for the intended purpose.

3. PROCESSING BY A LOCAL AI MODEL

When Ollama and the AI model run on the same computer, documents need not be
sent to an external provider. Their contents are still passed to the model,
analyzed, and used to generate a result. Local execution alone does not
determine whether such processing is permitted.

Temporary files, caches, reports, backups, and other software on the computer
may retain or expose data.

4. PROCESSING BY A REMOTE AI SERVICE

After selecting OpenAI, Anthropic, a remote Ollama, or another network service,
document page images leave the computer and are transmitted to a remote service.
This also applies to a service inside an organization's network. Data may be
processed or retained under the service operator's terms, safeguards, privacy
policies, and jurisdiction.

The installer requires four separate acknowledgements: the educational nature
of the program, using only sample documents without personal or sensitive data,
and transferring document contents to a local or remote AI model.
